# 🏋️‍♂️ Fitness Hub - Gym & Diet Website

## 🌟 Overview
Fitness Hub is a **modern and interactive website** designed to help individuals lead a healthier lifestyle by offering **personalized protein intake recommendations, workout routines, diet plans, and healthy recipes** for both vegetarians and non-vegetarians.

## 🎯 Features
✅ **Protein Calculator** – Determines daily protein needs based on weight, activity level, and diet.  
✅ **Workout Programs** – Tailored fitness plans for beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels.  
✅ **Diet Plans** – Nutritional guidance for vegetarians and non-vegetarians.  
✅ **Healthy Recipes** – High-protein meal suggestions.  
✅ **Fitness Blog** – Expert articles on wellness, motivation, and nutrition.
